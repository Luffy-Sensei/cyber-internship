--- AWARENESS TRAINING SCENARIO ---


PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- [HIGH] authority: The sender impersonates internal IT Security at Sqrock.
  Defense: Verify suspicious IT alerts via direct out-of-band communication channels.
- [HIGH] urgency: Imposes an artificial 24-hour verification deadline to induce pressure.
  Defense: Pause and evaluate security actions regardless of stated deadlines.
- [MEDIUM] fear: Threatens account suspension to compromise recipient judgment.
  Defense: Adhere to standard corporate authentication practices during urgent notices.
- [MEDIUM] personalization: Leverages recipient metadata including name, employer (Sqrock), and location (Bangalore, India).
  Defense: Recognize that open-source intelligence (OSINT) allows attackers to customize phishing hooks.

RED FLAGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- [HIGH] Sender impersonation: The message presents itself as an IT/security communication.
- [HIGH] Unexpected security notification: An unexpected account-security message should be independently verified.
- [HIGH] Urgency: A 24-hour deadline encourages rapid action.
- [MEDIUM] Threat of consequences: Account suspension is presented as the consequence of not complying.
- [HIGH] Verification request: The recipient is encouraged to follow a supplied verification path.
- [MEDIUM] Personalized hook: Publicly observable personal information is used to increase credibility.

DEFENSIVE GUIDANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Verify independently: Contact IT/security through an official internal channel rather than using contact information provided by the suspicious message.
- Do not trust the display name: A familiar-looking sender name does not prove that the message originated from the claimed organization.
- Avoid unexpected links: Navigate to known official services independently instead of following suspicious verification links.
- Do not allow urgency to bypass procedure: Security procedures should remain consistent even when a message claims immediate action is required.
- Report suspicious messages: Forward suspicious messages to the organization's security or phishing-reporting process.

EMAIL AUTHENTICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SPF: Checks whether the sending infrastructure is authorized to send mail for the claimed domain.
- DKIM: Uses a cryptographic signature to provide domain-level message authentication and integrity information.
- DMARC: Uses domain policy and alignment checks involving SPF and/or DKIM and provides reporting capabilities.

Note: Email authentication improves protection against domain impersonation but does not eliminate every form of phishing.
